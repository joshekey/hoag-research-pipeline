Help

Images to research

  1. Connect sources and choose folders under Sources. Index or reindex; follow Processing jobs.
  2. Open a study in Images. Check the selected patient and examination banner and inspect images.
  3. Reports searches TXT and SQL independently. Edit or clear the suggested last name, preview a record, compare its identity with DICOM, and explicitly associate it.
  4. Prepare the associated report in Anonymize & review. Inspect the sanitized report and images, then approve.
  5. Choose the output share in Export and export the approved study.

Reports can be searched without a selected study. Missing or conflicting report hints do not hide previews. Clinical relationship confirmation and privacy approval are separate. Conflicting DICOM identities, changed sources, active processing jobs, and unavailable mounts must be resolved before preparation/export.

SQL and reindexing

Scan the source folders containing the SQL dump first. In Sources, select the dump and click Rebuild SQL report index. Supported dump tables are labdataex, labdata, enc, and items. An unavailable SQL index is an error, not zero reports. SQL rebuild failures retain the prior index; successful rebuilds require searching and associating current references again.

Reindex DICOM + TXT rereads the last successful folder scope and clears prior approvals. Completed exported packages remain. Source shares are read-only.

Administration

Operational functions are separate from clinical review. Source mounts and bulk indexing can change the active research catalog. Access only when needed.

Connect or update a Windows share

Mount changes require an idle worker and invalidate prior approvals. Sources connect read-only; research output connects writable. Existing mounted shares will be reconnected.

Credentials are saved in root-only files on this server. Use hospital-trusted HTTPS. Only the three standard mountpoints can be managed here.
