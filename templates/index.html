Help & user guide

Return to DICOM studies

Start here: review a DICOM examination

  1. Open DICOM Studies and search by permitted study identifier, accession, date or modality.
  2. Click Review for the examination. Confirm the selected-study banner and displayed image count.
  3. In Correlating reports, review indexed TXT results and separate SQL candidate results. Click Reconcile report sources to repeat the current study's lookup.
  4. Open any available report preview and compare patient identity, exam/service date, modality and anatomy with the DICOM metadata. Conflicts or missing evidence require investigation.
  5. Open OHIF to examine original DICOM images and scroll the series. Inspect every relevant frame for privacy risks; merely opening the viewer does not mark review complete.
  6. Only use preparation/approval actions that apply to the selected report source. Approval and export require distinct review steps.

What report statuses mean

No indexed TXT match
No eligible report was associated by the indexed TXT matcher. It does not mean there is no SQL or historical report.
SQL candidates available
The separate SQL broker found possible reports for the selected examination. They are not verified associations.
SQL search not completed / unavailable
SQL has not been checked or its service returned an error; this does not mean zero matching reports.
Study identity conflict
The system detected inconsistent indexed identities. Stop and investigate rather than overriding the conflict.
Historical EMR research
A separate, access-controlled research catalog. Historical candidates do not automatically follow the selected active study.
Prepared vs. approved
A prepared report is a redaction draft, not evidence that DICOM images or the report have received final review.

When to reconcile

Use Reconcile report sources after opening or switching studies, if an SQL source was recently made available, or if a report candidate appears missing. It checks this study only and does not reindex DICOM, modify associations, or authorize export.

Common problems

Study or report unavailable / conflicting study identity: The selected study may be missing, the active TXT report may be unavailable, or the study state may indicate identity conflict. Preparing SQL/historical candidate text in the TXT preparation workflow is unsupported.

OHIF Something went wrong: Reopen the study and report the error category or HTTP status to support staff. Do not paste names, UIDs, medical reports or identifiable image URLs into external support tickets.

No SQL results: Distinguish a completed zero-result lookup from a broker error. If the broker is unavailable, contact a HOAG administrator.

Administration & security

Source shares are read-only. Mounting, indexing, bulk jobs, and schema inspection are operational tools available under Administration. Never interpret a candidate score as a clinical probability. Manual association, de-identification, image review, approval and export are independent.
